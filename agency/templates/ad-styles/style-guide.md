# THALO ad-style guide

Four visual directions locked for the launch campaign. Each style is a mood and
a set of craft decisions — never a layout to copy pixel-for-pixel. The product
must always be reused from REF01 (`730d6cbd-4daa-4fd1-9850-8f734ba5151d`,
see [clients/thalo/context/product-images/README.md](../../../clients/thalo/context/product-images/README.md))
so bottle geometry and the Deep Moss label stay identical across every asset.

Shared constraints: model `nano_banana_pro`, resolution `2k`, label text locked
to the three lines THALO / RICE GLOW SERUM / 30 ml (no other on-bottle text).

## STYLE-A — Morning Ritual

Reference: [thalo-STYLE-A.png](thalo-STYLE-A.png)

- **Composition.** The bottle sits slightly right of centre on a honed limestone
  vanity top, placed on the lower vertical third. A loose oatmeal-linen towel
  drapes to its right, a small bone-white ceramic dish and a slender eucalyptus
  branch sit back-left. The upper half is bathed in diffused wall light with
  soft shadow bars from a window — intentionally quiet, lived-in.
- **Lighting.** Soft morning window light raking from camera-left at ~45°,
  4500K warm daylight, large sheer-curtain diffusion. Gentle bounce fill from
  camera-right keeps shadows luminous. Only a tight contact shadow pools under
  the bottle; the window-bar shadow pattern falls across the back wall.
- **Camera angle.** Slight low-eye-level, ~5° above the shelf plane, 50mm
  standard lens, aperture ≈ f/2.8. Shallow DoF — bottle and label tack-sharp,
  wall and far props softly dissolve.
- **Palette.**
  `#F6F2EA` Rice White (wall and dish) · `#E7DED0` pale limestone ·
  `#D8CBB4` warm linen · `#B9C8B5` Sage Mist (bottle) · `#3F5143` Deep Moss
  (label and leaves).
- **Props.** Honed limestone shelf, loosely folded oatmeal linen towel, one
  slender branch of eucalyptus/ficus, one small bone-white ceramic dish, sheer
  curtain diffusing sunlight off-frame.
- **Emotion.** Serene, intimate, slow morning. "Da được nghỉ ngơi."
- **Copy zones.**
  - Upper-left quadrant (back wall above the branch) — cleanest area for a
    headline in Deep Moss or Rice White.
  - Lower strip under the bottle base, across the limestone shelf — room for a
    one-line claim + CTA.
  - Avoid the right third (towel texture) and the bottle silhouette.
- **Placements.** Feed 4:5 (native), carousel opener, reel cover. Reframe to
  3:4 for Pinterest. Not strong at 9:16 unless the window-light wall above is
  re-generated taller.
- **Prompt recipe.**
  ```
  Mode: lifestyle-scene · preset: bathroom-vanity · ratio: 4:5
  Product: REF01, upright, label facing camera, lower-right third
  Scene: honed pale-limestone vanity, sheer-curtain window off camera-left,
         soft shadow of window bars on the back wall
  Props: oatmeal-linen towel loosely folded back-right, bone-white ceramic
         dish + eucalyptus/ficus branch back-left (traces of presence, no
         people in frame)
  Light: 45° camera-left morning window, 4500K, sheer diffusion, soft bounce
         right, tight contact shadow only
  Lens: 50mm, f/2.8, shallow DoF, tack-sharp on bottle
  Palette: Rice White #F6F2EA, pale limestone, Sage Mist #B9C8B5,
           Deep Moss #3F5143
  Mood: calm Scandinavian commercial, airy negative space upper-left
  Append universal + anti-text-warp + anti-stock-feel negatives
  ```

## STYLE-B — Rice Minimal

Reference: [thalo-STYLE-B.png](thalo-STYLE-B.png)

- **Composition.** Bottle dead-centre on a low round pale-stone pedestal in the
  lower third. A loose diagonal arc of individual rice grains floats around the
  bottle from upper-right down to lower-left, each grain frozen mid-air with
  its own tiny shadow. Deliberately surreal, sculptural, gallery-clean.
- **Lighting.** Single hard key from camera-upper-left at ~45° high, gridded
  softbox, 5000K neutral daylight. One sharp-edged shadow rakes right across
  the backdrop. Subtle fill keeps the shadow side readable; sharp catchlights
  sit on the bulb and on every airborne grain.
- **Camera angle.** Straight-on at bottle mid-height, slight ~5° downward tilt
  on the pedestal, 85mm portrait lens, aperture ≈ f/8. Deep DoF — bottle,
  pedestal, and every grain are tack-sharp.
- **Palette.**
  `#B9C8B5` Sage Mist (seamless backdrop) · `#CBD6C4` lighter Sage (frosted
  glass) · `#EFE7D6` Rice White grains · `#D8CBB4` pale honed stone pedestal ·
  `#3F5143` Deep Moss (label).
- **Props.** One low round honed-stone pedestal, ~9 individual long-grain rice
  grains suspended in a diagonal arc. Nothing else.
- **Emotion.** Premium, poetic, deliberate. "Nước vo gạo, nhẹ tênh."
- **Copy zones.**
  - Upper-left (above the rice arc) — cleanest headline zone on flat Sage Mist.
  - Lower band under the pedestal — logo lock-up or CTA bar.
  - Right half above the shadow is usable only for small Deep Moss text; busy
    with floating grains otherwise.
- **Placements.** Feed 4:5 (native), 1:1 for IG grid and e-commerce, 9:16 by
  extending the backdrop vertically. Strongest for brand/premium posts,
  product-of-the-day, and launch announcements.
- **Prompt recipe.**
  ```
  Mode: conceptual-product · preset: sculptural-arrangement + floating-elements
  Ratio: 4:5 (or 1:1 / 9:16 — extend flat backdrop)
  Product: REF01, upright, centred, label front
  Pedestal: low round honed-stone, lower third, grounded contact shadow
  Floating elements: ~9 individual rice grains suspended in a diagonal arc
    from upper-right to lower-left, each sharply focused with its own micro-shadow
  Background: seamless flat Sage Mist #B9C8B5, subtle lower-third gradient
  Light: single hard key 45° upper-left, gridded softbox, 5000K, sharp
    rightward shadow, subtle right fill
  Lens: 85mm, f/8, deep DoF, CGI-render clarity
  Palette: Sage Mist #B9C8B5, Sage #CBD6C4, Rice White #EFE7D6, Deep Moss #3F5143
  Mood: premium minimal, fully committed surreal
  Append universal + anti-text-warp negatives + "no visible wires or supports"
  ```

## STYLE-C — Dewy Texture

Reference: [thalo-STYLE-C.png](thalo-STYLE-C.png)

- **Composition.** Bottle lies on its side on the surface of a shallow pool,
  tilted lower-left to upper-right, slightly below centre. Concentric ripples
  radiate outward. Beaded water droplets catch light on the frosted glass. The
  label faces upward and remains legible on the near side (expect minor refraction
  distortion at the far edge).
- **Lighting.** Soft cool overcast skylight from above and slightly behind
  camera, 6000K, large softbox-quality diffusion. Sharp clean catchlights on
  the droplets and on the glass shoulder. A soft under-bottle shadow ripples on
  the pale pool floor.
- **Camera angle.** Three-quarter top-down, ~45° above the water plane, 100mm
  macro lens, aperture ≈ f/4. Shallow-to-medium DoF — bottle sharp, outer
  ripples soft.
- **Palette.**
  `#D8E0DA` pale aqua water surface · `#EFE7D6` Rice White pool floor ·
  `#B9C8B5` Sage Mist (bottle) · `#3F5143` Deep Moss (label).
- **Props.** Shallow still fresh water, pale bone-white sand/stone pool floor,
  scattered beaded droplets on the glass. No other objects.
- **Emotion.** Fresh, dewy, lightweight hydration. "Nhẹ tênh, cấp ẩm."
- **Copy zones.**
  - Top strip above the outermost ripple — calm water surface, great for a
    headline in Deep Moss.
  - Bottom-left corner — quiet water, room for CTA / price tag.
  - Avoid directly over the ripples (busy refraction).
- **Placements.** Feed 4:5 and 1:1 for texture/claim posts. Strongest as a
  **video still** — ripples and droplets animate beautifully in 9:16 reels and
  TikToks (loop the ripple, settle on the bottle). Reframe from 4:5 master by
  cropping top strip for 1:1.
- **Prompt recipe.**
  ```
  Mode: conceptual-product · preset: surreal-environment (water surface)
  Ratio: 4:5
  Product: REF01, lying on its side on water surface, tilted lower-left to
    upper-right, label facing up and legible on the near side
  Scene: shallow still fresh water fills the frame, pale bone-white pool
    floor just visible beneath, no shoreline, no pool edges
  Surreal element: bottle rests impossibly on the surface (not submerging),
    concentric ripples radiate out, beaded water droplets on the glass
  Light: soft cool overcast from above-behind camera, 6000K, large diffusion,
    sharp catchlights on droplets, soft rippling under-shadow
  Lens: 100mm macro, f/4, tack-sharp focus on bottle and label, soft ripples
  Palette: pale aqua #D8E0DA, Rice White #EFE7D6, Sage Mist #B9C8B5,
    Deep Moss #3F5143
  Mood: cool, fresh, premium, hydrating
  Append universal + anti-text-warp negatives + "no half-submerged bottle,
    no violent splash, no visible pool walls"
  ```

## STYLE-D — In Hand

Reference: [thalo-STYLE-D.png](thalo-STYLE-D.png)

- **Composition.** Tight closeup, bottle held upright by one adult woman's hand
  extended toward the camera. Bottle occupies ~50% of frame, placed on the
  right vertical third. Fingers curl around the back and side; thumb rests on
  the near face below the label. Background is a soft cream-linen top in shallow
  bokeh — no face, wrist to fingertips only.
- **Lighting.** Soft window light from camera-left at ~45°, 5000K daylight
  neutral, large softbox-quality diffusion, gentle right fill. Clean catchlight
  on the glass shoulder and the matte bulb. Skin highlights are natural with
  visible micro-texture.
- **Camera angle.** Eye-level, bottle front-on, 85mm portrait lens, aperture
  ≈ f/2.8. Shallow DoF — bottle and label tack-sharp, hand slightly softer at
  the knuckles, background dissolves.
- **Palette.**
  `#F6F2EA` Rice White · `#E6D8C5` cream linen (background) ·
  `#C9A98A` warm natural skin · `#B9C8B5` Sage Mist (bottle) ·
  `#3F5143` Deep Moss (label).
- **Props.** None beyond the hand, the bottle, and a soft cream-linen top as
  the implied wearer. Bare wrist, no jewelry, short unpainted natural nails.
- **Emotion.** Warm, honest, human. "Bạn — thật, không giả bộ."
- **Copy zones.**
  - Upper-left corner above the thumb — cleanest zone, warm beige blur.
  - Lower-left corner below the wrist — second-best zone for a CTA.
  - Never cover the label or the fingers; the hand carries the trust signal.
- **Placements.** Feed 4:5 (native) for testimonial-style posts, "my routine"
  carousels, review captions. **9:16 reel cover** by generating a vertical
  version (same recipe, taller crop around the forearm). Weakest at 1:1 — the
  gesture loses its reach.
- **Prompt recipe.**
  ```
  Mode: closeup-product-with-person · preset: hand-holding-vertical
  Ratio: 4:5 (9:16 for reel cover — extend forearm downward)
  Product: REF01, upright, label fully legible, right vertical third,
    ~50% of visible area
  Person: one adult Vietnamese woman's hand, warm natural skin, short
    unpainted nails, no jewelry, no face in frame, mid-forearm crop
  Interaction: hand cradles the bottle and extends it gently toward camera,
    relaxed grip, thumb on near side below the label
  Light: soft window 45° camera-left, 5000K, large diffusion, gentle right
    fill, clean catchlight on glass and bulb
  Lens: 85mm, f/2.8, shallow DoF, creamy background bokeh
  Palette: Rice White #F6F2EA, cream linen #E6D8C5, warm skin #C9A98A,
    Sage Mist #B9C8B5, Deep Moss #3F5143
  Mood: warm, honest, tactile beauty editorial
  Append universal + anti-uncanny + anti-text-warp negatives +
    "no painted nails, no rings or bracelets, no second hand"
  ```

## Rules

- These four files are **style inspiration, not layouts to copy**. Treat them
  as a mood/palette/lighting reference. Composition should vary per deliverable
  (feed vs. story vs. video cover), framing should change, props can be
  substituted within the palette.
- The product in every downstream asset is **always REF01**
  (`730d6cbd-4daa-4fd1-9850-8f734ba5151d`). Pass it as `medias[].value` with
  role `image_references`. Do not re-generate the bottle from scratch; do not
  invent a new label; do not alter the three locked lines.
- Palette stays inside THALO brand tokens — Rice White `#F6F2EA`,
  Sage Mist `#B9C8B5`, Deep Moss `#3F5143`, Warm Clay `#D9A88A`. Style-specific
  neutrals above (limestone, linen, aqua, skin) are allowed support tones, not
  replacements.
- Vietnamese on-image copy must carry correct diacritics and respect the
  [agency quality bar](../../context/quality-bar.md) banned-words list. English
  on-image copy is only allowed when the brief explicitly calls for it.
- First generate without any text overlay (as these style refs are), then add
  Vietnamese copy in post or via a typography pass. Do not ask the model to
  render marketing claims.
