# Quality bar — every deliverable must pass

## Visuals
- Product label readable, spelled exactly as in the client's product.md
- Bottle/pack shape, cap and colour match the approved product reference images
- No extra or distorted fingers, faces or objects
- No third-party brand names, logos or recognisable products
- On-image text (Vietnamese) has correct diacritics; if not, remove the text

## Video
- Hook in the first 1 second
- Product visible within the first 3 seconds
- No warped label during motion; if warped, regenerate that shot

## Copy (Vietnamese)
- Matches the client's brand voice
- Cosmetic claims only. NEVER: "trị mụn", "trị nám", "chữa", "điều trị",
  "hết mụn sau X ngày", "trắng bật tông", "100%", "số 1", medical or
  before/after promises
- Allowed style: "giúp da trông rạng rỡ hơn", "cảm giác mềm mượt",
  "hỗ trợ cấp ẩm"
- No fake reviews, fake testimonials or invented statistics
