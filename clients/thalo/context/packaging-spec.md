  Line 3: 30 ml            (smallest, at the bottom)
  No other text, no barcode, no icons on the front.
- Box (optional in shots): matte Rice White (#F6F2EA) carton, same 3 lines in Deep Moss
- Must stay identical in every image: bottle proportions, frosted sage glass,
  white dropper, the 3 label lines exactly as written above
