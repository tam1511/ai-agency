## Why this order

- **SH01 leads (Day 2, one day after launch static).** The 8-second loop is the algorithmically fastest asset in the set — it maximises TikTok's loop metric and reaches the broadest cross-segment audience earliest. The pain-point hook (`Mặt bóng lúc 11 giờ sáng?`) speaks to Segment B and Segment C simultaneously, seeding brand awareness before the longer-form videos land. SH01 also carries the offer, so Day 2 audiences see the launch price immediately after the Day 1 static announcement.

- **S03 on Day 1 sets the launch tone without leading with video.** A feed static as the launch post is a deliberate pacing choice — it lets the brand's visual identity (STYLE-A limestone vanity, Deep Moss wordmark, Warm Clay offer badge) establish before TikTok's algorithm begins testing the video assets. It also ensures the offer is communicated to FB and IG audiences who may not catch SH01.

- **V02 drops Day 4, after SH01 has established hook recognition.** Viewers who saw SH01 on Day 2 will recognise the STYLE-C water visual and the `thấm nhanh` language when V02 appears. Familiarity lowers the scroll-away threshold. V02 is the primary conversion asset (Segment B, absorption demo, offer footer) — it performs better when the brand has had two days of feed presence.

- **V01 drops Day 8, mid-week after both STYLE-C videos have run.** By Day 8 the audience has been primed on the texture and offer story. V01's educational ingredient cadence (three-beat skintellectual breakdown) is best received by Segment A viewers who have already seen the brand twice and are now ready to go deeper. Dropping it before V02 would waste the trust-building that V02's visual does.

- **S04 on Day 7 bridges V02 and V01.** The ingredient story static carries the offer (touchpoint 3) and frames the ingredient content that V01 will deliver in video form the next day. It acts as a primer for Segment A before the video drops.

- **V03 drops Day 11, well-separated from V02.** Seven days between V02 and V03 ensures the STYLE-C end-card (V02) and the STYLE-A end-card (V03) are not seen back-to-back by the same viewer, satisfying virality.md §3b. V03's GRWM magic-realism format is a tonal shift — it works best as a third video encounter, when the audience has formed enough brand familiarity to find the floating-dropper moment charming rather than confusing.

- **S06 + SH01 re-post close the offer window (Days 13–14).** The soft reminder (`Còn vài ngày nữa`) lands in the final 48 hours without urgency language. SH01 is re-posted on Day 14 (the final offer day) because its 8-second loop will generate the most repeat views on a closing day — and the offer pill is baked into the visual without needing copy to do extra work.

- **Static rest days (Days 6, 10) protect team capacity and cadence.** Dropping a feed static between high-effort video days gives the small team a low-production-cost slot while maintaining daily feed presence. STYLE-C statics on those days keep the visual family consistent and reinforce the absorption/texture narrative between video drops.
