## Offer-touchpoint note

The 329.000đ launch offer appears in captions for **S03, S04, S06, V02, and SH01** — the five assets designated as offer-touchpoints in the shot list and campaign concept §6. These five assets are the ones that visually carry the Warm Clay offer badge or footer strip, so the caption copy reinforces what the viewer already sees on screen. The remaining five assets (S01, S02, S05, V01, V03) do not carry the offer in copy or visual — this keeps the offer feeling earned and contextual, not omnipresent. All captions have been audited against the banned-word list from brand.md and quality-bar.md before writing.

Banned words checked and confirmed absent from all captions: `trị, chữa, thần thánh, trắng bật tông, cam kết 100%, số 1 Việt Nam, trị mụn, trị nám, hết mụn sau X ngày, cháy hàng, flash sale, cuối cùng, 100%`.
