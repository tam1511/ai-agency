# THALO Rice Glow Serum — Campaign Concept
**Client:** THALO | **Date:** 2026-11-15 | **Author:** Creative Strategist
**Window:** 14-day launch, TikTok + Instagram + Facebook
**Goal recap:** awareness among oily/combo-skin women 22–32 in HCMC + Hanoi, first 1,000 orders on TikTok Shop (brief.md §Result we want).

---

## 1. Big idea

**Nước gạo của bà, nâng cấp cho làn da đang sống trong nắng Sài Gòn – Hà Nội.**

One sentence: THALO reframes a household Vietnamese beauty ritual (nước vo gạo) as a modern, lightweight serum built for the one condition no imported serum speaks to — urban humid-climate oily skin.

**Why this beats the competitor gaps (research/market-research.md §5 Opportunity Gaps):**
- Gap 1 — No serum in the 250k–550k band currently leads with fermented rice as hero alongside niacinamide + HA. We claim that white space first.
- Gap 2 — Category is loud with "thần thánh / trắng bật tông" language; a calm, honest, chemist-friend voice is a moat (84% of VN consumers prioritise ingredient transparency, §5 Gap 2).
- Gap 3 — Every competitor positions globally ("all skin types," "K-beauty glow"). Nobody speaks to the 11am T-zone shine on a Saigon commute. We own that moment.

## 3. Three angles

Each angle is a different lens on the big idea, aimed at one audience micro-segment from research §1 Audience Segments. All three fit on the same shelf and feed one coherent 14-day story.

### Angle 1 — "Thành phần, nói thẳng" (Ingredient, told straight)

- **Audience:** Segment A — the Ingredient-Literate Student, 22–26, HCMC, cross-checks on INCI Decoder (research §1 Segment A).
- **Hook idea (VN):** `Rice ferment + Niacinamide 5% + HA — mình nói đúng những gì có trong lọ.`
- **Core message:** THALO's three actives, explained calmly, no miracle words. One ingredient per beat.
- **Proof point (product.md):** "Rice ferment filtrate, Niacinamide 5%, Hyaluronic acid" — exact wording from product.md §Key ingredients. Benefit wording stays inside product.md §Allowed benefit wording ("giúp da trông rạng rỡ hơn… hỗ trợ cấp ẩm").
- **Why it works:** Segment A fears "thần thánh" marketing (P6, research §2); P5 shows they choose products from ingredient lists, not copy. Honest naming of % and actives converts this micro-segment fastest.
- **Format fit:** TikTok **Format 2 — Skintellectual Ingredient Breakdown** (research §4). 30–60 s calm delivery, on-screen text, no hard sell. Supports Gap 2.

### Angle 2 — "Sáng ở văn phòng, chiều ra phố" (Bright at the office, out on the street by evening)

- **Audience:** Segment B — the Pragmatic Office Worker, 26–32, HCMC + Hanoi, 9h AC + humid commute (research §1 Segment B).
- **Hook idea (VN):** `Mặt bóng lúc 11h sáng? Lớp serum mỏng này thấm trong vài giây.`
- **Core message:** A lightweight, non-sticky serum that disappears under makeup and doesn't add to the "bí da" feeling.
- **Proof point (product.md):** "Texture: light, watery, absorbs in seconds, no sticky finish" + "lightweight in humid weather" (product.md §Allowed benefit wording).
- **Why it works:** Directly answers P1 (summer oiliness trap), P3 (layering feels suffocating — "bí da"), P4 (fast-absorbing non-sticky is top priority) and P7 (humid weather enemy) in research §2. Segment B fears a 400k serum making their T-zone worse — we show the opposite on camera.
- **Format fit:** TikTok **Format 1 — Sensory Absorption Demo** (research §4, highest conversion in category). 10–20 s texture + absorption on hand, ASMR, text overlay names the hero.

### Angle 3 — "Thói quen 3 giây" (The 3-second habit)

- **Audience:** Segment C — the Trend-Alert Shopper, 22–29, TikTok Shop-first, follows micro-KOCs (research §1 Segment C).
- **Hook idea (VN):** `2–3 giọt sau toner. Xong. Ngày của bạn bắt đầu.`
- **Core message:** THALO slots into a real morning routine without adding steps; the launch price makes trying it a tiny decision.
- **Proof point (product.md):** "Use: AM and PM, 2–3 drops after toner, before moisturiser." Launch offer 329.000đ for 14 days (product.md §Price).
- **Why it works:** Segment C buys impulsively during launches and flash sales, fears paying full price when a promo is about to drop (research §1 Segment C). A low-friction routine + a time-bound offer compresses their decision window. P5 (visible results, 4-week user quote) backs the long-term trust after the quick try-in.
- **Format fit:** TikTok **Format 3 — GRWM Morning Routine** (research §4, 40% higher completion vs. static). 30–45 s, product shown post-toner, soft ambient audio, natural morning light.

## 4. Tone of voice (this campaign)

Per brand.md §Personality and §Voice:
- Calm, honest, a little poetic, never preachy. "Friend who studied chemistry."
- Xưng **THALO** or **tụi mình**; address the viewer as **bạn**.
- Short, warm sentences. No hyperbole. No promises of results.
- Max one emoji per caption, from the approved set: 🌾 💧 ✨.
- Sanctioned words to lean on: `rạng rỡ, mịn, nhẹ tênh, dịu, nước vo gạo, làn da được nghỉ ngơi` (brand.md §Words we use).
- Banned, never used: `trị, chữa, thần thánh, trắng bật tông, cam kết 100%, số 1 Việt Nam` (brand.md §Words we never use). Also honours quality-bar.md §Copy — no "trị mụn/nám", "hết mụn sau X ngày", "100%", "số 1", no fake reviews, cosmetic claims only.
- Campaign-specific nuance: because Angle 1 is ingredient-led, numbers (5% niacinamide) and clinical neutral language are allowed; but we always close the beat with a sensory word ("nhẹ tênh", "dịu") so the chemistry stays warm.

## 5. Visual style choice

**Primary: STYLE-A — Morning Ritual.**
**Secondary / fallback: STYLE-C — Dewy Texture** (for all texture/claim moments and the short-form video surface).

**Why these, not the others:**

- **STYLE-A (Morning Ritual) as primary** directly dramatises Gap 3 — the HCMC/Hanoi morning that competitors ignore. Soft window light on limestone + a slow intimate "da được nghỉ ngơi" mood (style-guide.md STYLE-A §Emotion) is the exact aesthetic of Format 3 (GRWM Morning Routine) and anchors Angle 3. The upper-left copy zone leaves clean space for the price badge during launch-offer posts. Best placement: **feed 4:5** for all three angles, and the vertical reframe is called out as a known constraint in the style guide — we accept that and solve it in production, not here.
- **STYLE-C (Dewy Texture) as secondary** is purpose-built to sell the P4 pain point (thấm nhanh, không dính rít) and the lightweight/humid-weather positioning in Angle 2. The style-guide explicitly notes it is "strongest as a video still — ripples and droplets animate beautifully in 9:16 reels and TikToks" (style-guide.md STYLE-C §Placements). This is the ideal surface for Format 1 (Sensory Absorption Demo), our highest-converting format. Best placement: **9:16 story + video + short** (V02, SH01), plus any texture-claim static.

**Why not STYLE-B (Rice Minimal):** beautiful, sculptural, but gallery-cold. It would read as another premium K-import — exactly the register research §3 flags as the thing we're trying NOT to be (Gap 2, honest/calm voice over premium coldness). Keep in back pocket for a later brand/hero post if needed; not a launch workhorse.

**Why not STYLE-D (In Hand):** strong trust signal and a natural fit for Angle 2 testimonial beats, but the launch window is only 14 days and we need maximum consistency; introducing a human hand asset also raises the quality-bar.md §Visuals risk ("no extra or distorted fingers"). We'd rather lock to two style families now and optionally revisit STYLE-D in a post-launch extension.

**Placement map (for the shot list stage, not yet written):**
- Feed 4:5 statics S01–S03 → STYLE-A primary, one per angle.
- Story 9:16 statics S04–S06 → STYLE-A extended vertical for Angles 1 and 3; STYLE-C for Angle 2 (texture claim).
- Videos V01–V03 (9:16) → STYLE-A for V01 (Angle 1) and V03 (Angle 3); STYLE-C for V02 (Angle 2 absorption demo).
- Short SH01 (9:16) → cut from the best video; if V02 wins, carries STYLE-C forward.

Palette is fixed by brand.md §Colours (Rice White #F6F2EA, Sage Mist #B9C8B5, Deep Moss #3F5143, Warm Clay #D9A88A) and reinforced by the style-guide. Warm Clay is reserved for CTA badges + the 329.000đ launch-offer tag; it never contaminates the serene scene colour.

## 6. Launch offer plan — 329.000đ across 14 days

Brief requires the 329.000đ offer in **≥3 posts** without feeling pushy (brief.md §Must). Plan to place it in **4 touchpoints** so the count is safe even if one post is reworked. Count-level only — marketing-ops writes the actual calendar.

| # | Day window | Post type | Angle | Offer role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 1 (launch) | Static feed 4:5 (one of S01–S03) | Angle 3 (routine + offer) | Primary launch announcement. Warm Clay badge, lower-left copy zone (STYLE-A). Tone: "Hôm nay THALO ra mắt — 329.000đ, trong 14 ngày đầu." Honest framing, no urgency shouting. |
| 2 | Day 3–4 | Video V02 (absorption demo) | Angle 2 | Offer appears as a 1-second end card only — texture is the star; price is a quiet footer. |
| 3 | Day 7 | Story 9:16 (S04 or S05) | Angle 1 (ingredient breakdown) | Price lives below the ingredient list. Rationale: Segment A respects offers that come after education, not before. |
| 4 | Day 12–13 | Short SH01 + GRWM-style static | Angle 3 | Soft closing reminder — "Còn vài ngày nữa, mức ra mắt kết thúc." No countdown pressure language, no "cuối cùng". |

Why this cadence feels honest, not pushy:
- The offer is **always secondary to a content reason** (routine, texture, ingredient, reminder) — never a standalone sale post.
- It sits in the **copy zone the style already reserves** (STYLE-A upper-left or lower band; STYLE-C top strip or lower-left), so visually it never fights the serum.
- Language is cosmetic + factual, inside brand voice: "mức ra mắt", "trong 14 ngày đầu" — not "flash sale", "cháy hàng", "cam kết". No banned words from quality-bar.md §Copy appear.
