# Campaign concept

## Inputs
- clients/<client>/brief/brief.md
- clients/<client>/context/brand.md, product.md
- clients/<client>/research/market-research.md
- agency/templates/ad-styles/style-guide.md
- agency/context/quality-bar.md

## Steps
1. Propose 3 big ideas in one line each. Pick the strongest and say why
   (link it to a pain point or opportunity in the research).
2. Write clients/<client>/strategy/campaign-concept.md:
   - Big idea (1 sentence) + tagline in Vietnamese (max 6 words)
   - Why it works (3 bullets, each tied to research)
   - 3 angles: name, target segment, core message, proof point from product.md
   - Tone of voice notes for this campaign
   - Visual direction: which STYLE-A..D for which placement, colour use
   - Key messages allowed / phrases to avoid (from quality-bar.md and brand.md)
   - Storyboard for each of the 3 videos: 4-6 beats with seconds
     (beat 1 = hook in first 1 s, product visible by 3 s)
3. Return the big idea, tagline and 3 angle names to the CEO.

## Quality check
- Tagline is natural Vietnamese, no forbidden words
- Every angle maps to a research finding

## Never
- Make medical or before/after claims
- Generate media
