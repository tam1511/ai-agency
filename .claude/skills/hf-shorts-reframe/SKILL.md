# Short with on-screen text

## Inputs
- Approved videos in clients/<client>/assets/video/
- clients/<client>/strategy/shot-list.md (row SH01) and campaign-concept.md (tagline)
- clients/<client>/context/brand.md (colours, type)

## Steps
1. Pick the best approved video (ask the CEO if unsure).
2. If it is not 9:16, call Higgsfield reframe to 9:16, keeping the product in frame.
3. Load the Higgsfield workflow "video-editing" (get_workflow_instructions) and
   follow it to add animated text: hook line (0-2 s), one benefit line,
   tagline + CTA at the end. Brand colours Deep Moss / Rice White, thin sans-serif.
4. Download to assets/shorts/<file name>. Log it with credits used.

## Quality check
- Vietnamese diacritics correct, text inside safe area (not under TikTok UI)
- Each text line on screen at least 1.5 s

## Never
- Use copyrighted music
